# The big moments seen from outside: the stand-in second player ("Bystander") does them while the real client watches.
# Flat world, default profile. Each moment: mark the bystander's packet record, open a watch window on the real client
# (hotbar 1 with a long observe: an input that changes nothing), cause it, then list what the bystander was sent.
# Setup verbs used on purpose: bystander operate/form/fit/charge, hostify, gantry (these moments were done by real input
# on the player's own side in the earlier scripts; here only the watcher's side is under test).
@world flat
@gamemode survival
@observe 30
wait 40
/time set 6000
/gamerule doMobSpawning false
/effect give @s minecraft:resistance 999 4 true
report camera: six blocks from the chair, 45 degrees toward the patient's left front (the chair faces north: north-west of it)
/tp @s -3.74 -60 -0.74 -45 9
/setblock 0 -60 3 headinajar:separation_chair
wait 10
bystander spawn 2 -60 2 90
wait 20
report == 1. the operation, done by the bystander, watched from 8 blocks
bystander mark
hud off
observe 260
frames chair_op_outside 260
bystander operate 0 -60 3
wait 60
shot out_op_60
wait 80
shot out_op_140
wait 110
observe 30
bystander state
bystander packets sound|particle|actionbar|chat|payload|title
hud on
shot out_op_after
/tp @s 0.5 -60 -5.5 0 12
report == 2. a link break: the bystander's body is taken 80 blocks away; its head stays by the chair
bystander mark
observe 220
hotbar 1
bystander tp 0 -60 80
wait 210
observe 30
bystander state
bystander packets sound|particle|actionbar|chat|title
shot out_link_broken
report == 3. mounting a host: a sawn cow beside the head; the bystander (now a jar) right-clicks it
hostify minecraft:cow 3 -60 1
wait 10
report the bystander has no client, so its link quality never drops (try 1: still linked after 210 ticks at 77 blocks); it swaps into its head by the real Swap key payload instead
bystander tp 0 -60 0
bystander key swap
wait 20
bystander state
bystander tp 1.5 -60 1 -90 0
wait 5
look 30 15
bystander mark
bystander useentity nearest cow
wait 40
bystander state
bystander packets sound|particle|actionbar|chat|title|payload
shot out_host_mounted
view 1
wait 5
shot out_host_third_person
view 0
report == 4. Telekinesis lifting and throwing a cow, by the bystander's jar
bystander sneak on
wait 5
bystander sneak off
wait 10
bystander fit telekinesis,hop_springs
bystander charge 100000
/summon minecraft:cow 4.5 -60 -2.5 {NoAI:1b}
bystander tp 4.5 -60 0.5 180 -5
wait 10
look 60 10
bystander mark
bystander key ability
wait 30
shot out_tk_holding
bystander state
bystander key throw
wait 40
bystander packets sound|particle|actionbar|payload
inspect near cow 40
shot out_tk_thrown

gantry -6 -60 3 hopper

bystander useblock -6 -60 3
wait 10

inspect near hopper 20
shot out_mecha_assembled

wait 40
bystander state
bystander packets sound|particle|actionbar|chat|title|payload
shot out_mecha_boarded
