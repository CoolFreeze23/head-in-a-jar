# Run under both profiles (SMOKE_PROFILE unset, then pack): a jar with Telekinesis lifts a cow and throws it, by real
# keys. The jar form and the module are set with the `jar` and `fit` setup verbs (the department C journey did it by
# hand); this script compares only the keys. Under pack, R is both the ability and sprint, and attack is the right button.
@world flat
@gamemode survival
@observe 30
wait 40
/time set 6000
/gamerule doMobSpawning false
/effect give @s minecraft:resistance 999 4 true
/tp @s 0.5 -60 0.5 0 0
wait 10
jar
wait 20
fit telekinesis,hop_springs
charge 100000
wait 10
/summon minecraft:cow 0.5 -60 3.0 {NoAI:1b}
wait 10
lookat cow 0.9
wait 3
shot tk_before
inspect me
report == R (the ability key) at the cow
key ability
wait 30
inspect near cow 8
shot tk_holding
report == attack to throw
key attack
wait 40
inspect near cow 30
inspect me
shot tk_thrown
report == R with nothing in hand: walk forward first (does R also sprint?)
key forward down
wait 5
key ability
wait 20
key forward up
wait 10
inspect me
report == the Jar menu key
key menu
wait 15
shot tk_menu
press escape
wait 5
