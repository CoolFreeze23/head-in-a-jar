# The Assembly Gantry says no, then repairs. The stand-in second player presses Assemble with no frame (the head shakes
# at rest, the lamp red), builds a Bulwark, presses Assemble again with it standing on the pad (the head shakes stood
# off), then, the Bulwark damaged, presses Repair: the plating mends it and the battery is topped up at once, and the
# arm makes its weld pass with the charge lead out from the reel. Filmed from about six blocks at the front-left
# three-quarter (the reel's side), by day.
@observe 20
wait 40
/time set 6000
/gamerule doMobSpawning false
/gamerule doDaylightCycle false
/gamerule doWeatherCycle false
/kill @e[type=!player]
/clear @s
/setblock 0 -60 0 headinajar:assembly_gantry[facing=north]
/tp @s -3.8 -59.4 -3.8 -45 -1.7
bystander spawn 0 -60 -2 0
wait 20
hud off
bystander useblock 0 -60 0 north
wait 5
frames r1_refuse_rest 40
wait 4
bystander button 1
wait 40
bystander close
gantry 0 -60 0 bulwark
bystander useblock 0 -60 0 north
wait 3
bystander button 1
bystander close
wait 232
inspect near bulwark 4
bystander useblock 0 -60 0 north
wait 5
frames r2_refuse_loaded 40
wait 4
bystander button 1
wait 40
bystander close
/damage @e[type=headinajar:bulwark,limit=1] 60
wait 20
inspect near bulwark 4
bystander useblock 0 -60 0 north
wait 5
frames r3_repair 85
wait 4
bystander button 3
wait 2
bystander close
inspect near bulwark 4
wait 85
inspect near bulwark 4
inspect block 0 -60 0
report repair done
