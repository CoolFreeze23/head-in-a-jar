# Every hireable species with a staff fit, as workers: three takes (loop only; + Hard Hat; + carrying).
# The creatures are given a worker record by setup (data merge) so every family shows, flying and hostile ones too;
# hiring is not what this proves. The creatures of a take are dropped into the void and killed there before the next (a /kill
# on the board splits slimes into small ones that stand in the shots; left alive in the void, the old take's creatures
# would take the next take's data merges). Shots: front three-quarter (the creature's left) and side, at 5 blocks.
@world flat
@difficulty easy
@clean
time set 6000
weather clear
gamerule doDaylightCycle false
gamerule doMobSpawning false
hud off
# --- take: loop (in rain: undead burn at noon without a hat, and rain is the one vanilla thing that stops it without a helmet)
weather rain
tp @e[type=!player] 0 -400 0
kill @e[type=!player]
wait 10
wait 5
