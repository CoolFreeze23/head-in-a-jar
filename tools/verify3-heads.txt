# Third repair pass, real input: a stranger's knock on a placed head is refused; the head in a soul fire cracks a point a
# second and its owner is warned each time; a Glass Patch on your own placed head mends it; a head left in the fire
# breaks with its own death line. Default profile and SMOKE_PROFILE=pack.
@world flat
@gamemode survival
@difficulty easy
@observe 20
wait 40
/time set 6000
/gamerule doMobSpawning false
/gamerule doFireTick false
report == H1 the head set down 1.5 east of me; the bystander knocks on it (expect: its refusal payload, my glass still 10/10)
/tp @s 0.5 -60 0.5 -90 0
wait 5
separate
wait 20
inspect near head_jar 4
bystander spawn 2 -60 2 180
wait 10
bystander look 180 35
bystander mark
bystander attack nearest head_jar
wait 10
bystander packets payload|actionbar|sound
inspect me
bystander remove
report == H2 soul fire under the head (expect: actionbar Your head is in the fire, glass 9/10, then 8, 7; feedback.danger; glass 6-8 at the end)
/tp @s -6.5 -60 0.5 -90 10
wait 5
/setblock 2 -61 0 minecraft:soul_soil
/setblock 2 -60 0 minecraft:soul_fire
observe 75
key sneak down
wait 30
shot h2_fire_warning
wait 35
key sneak up
observe 20
inspect me
/setblock 2 -60 0 minecraft:air
wait 10
report == H3 a Glass Patch on my own placed head, by a real click (expect: glass +5, the head stays put)
/give @s headinajar:glass_patch 2
/tp @s 0.5 -60 0.5 -90 20
wait 5
hotbar 2
lookat head_jar
key use
wait 10
inspect me
inspect near head_jar 4
report == H4 the head left in the fire until it breaks (expect: warnings every two seconds, then the chat line left their head too near the fire, and the death screen)
/tp @s -6.5 -60 0.5 -90 10
wait 5
/setblock 2 -60 0 minecraft:soul_fire
observe 300
key sneak down
wait 290
key sneak up
observe 20
shot h4_death_screen
inspect me
