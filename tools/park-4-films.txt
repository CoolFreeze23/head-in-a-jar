# Parking filmed every frame from a fixed camera near the machine: a Hopper and a Wheelhouse each boarded, driven a
# little, got out of and left to park; the moment its look is kept is reported (parkstats: last kept at tick), to be
# found in the film's frames.csv and looked at frame by frame.
@observe 20
wait 60
/time set 6000
/gamerule doMobSpawning false
/gamerule doDaylightCycle false
/gamerule doWeatherCycle false
hud off
jar
charge 100000
mecha hopper 100 -60 100 hydraulic_legs,drill,night_optics
/tp @s 100.5 -60 97 0 0
wait 120
lookat headinajar:hopper
key use
wait 80
riding hopper
key forward hold 20
wait 20
watch at 3 1.5 -1 70 15
frames f_hopper_park 300
wait 5
sneak down
key jar_menu hold 25
sneak up
wait 300
parkstats
watch off
mecha wheelhouse 140 -60 100 hydraulic_legs
/tp @s 140.5 -60 96 0 0
wait 120
lookat headinajar:wheelhouse
key use
wait 80
riding wheelhouse
key forward hold 20
wait 20
watch at 4 2 -2 63 15
frames f_wheelhouse_park 300
wait 5
sneak down
key jar_menu hold 25
sneak up
wait 300
parkstats
watch off
