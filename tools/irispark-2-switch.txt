# Under a shader pack: a kept Hopper with the pack switched off and on again (its look is kept again in the game's own
# layout, then the pack's), then boarded, driven a little, got out of and left to park, filmed every frame from beside it
# with its shadow on the ground in view. Run with SMOKE_MODS=sodium,iris SMOKE_SHADER=BSLClassic_v10.0.zip.

wait 60
/time set 1500
/gamerule doMobSpawning false
/gamerule doDaylightCycle false
/gamerule doWeatherCycle false

wait 300
parkstats
shot w_kept
irisshaders off
wait 100
parkstats
shot w_shaders_off
irisshaders on
wait 300
parkstats

lookat headinajar:hopper
key use
wait 80
riding hopper
parkstats

wait 20
watch at 3 1.5 -1 70 15
frames f_hopper_park 300
