# The big moments from outside, second take. Flat world, survival, default profile.
# The bystander is the doer where it can be (it has no client, so it cannot break its own link: there the real client
# is the doer and the bystander's packet record is what a watching player is sent).
# Setup verbs: bystander form/fit/charge, gantry (stocks the Gantry), separate, fit, charge.
@world flat
@gamemode survival
@observe 30
wait 40
/time set 6000
/gamerule doMobSpawning false
/effect give @s minecraft:resistance 999 4 true
report == 1. a mecha: the bystander assembles a Hopper at a stocked Gantry, then boards it as a jar
/setblock -6 -60 3 headinajar:assembly_gantry
gantry -6 -60 3 hopper
bystander spawn -4 -60 1 90
wait 10
bystander tp -4 -60 1 90 30
/tp @s 2.5 -60 -4.5 50 10
wait 5
bystander mark
bystander useblock -6 -60 3
wait 10
bystander state
observe 80
bystander button 1
wait 80
observe 30
bystander packets sound|particle|actionbar|chat|title|payload
inspect near hopper 20
shot o2_mecha_assembled
bystander close
bystander form jar
wait 20
bystander state
/execute at @e[type=headinajar:hopper,limit=1] run tp @e[type=player,name=Bystander] ~1.5 ~ ~ 90 20
wait 5
bystander state
bystander mark
observe 60
bystander useentity nearest hopper
wait 60
observe 30
bystander state
bystander packets sound|particle|actionbar|chat|title|payload
shot o2_mecha_boarded
view 1
wait 5
shot o2_mecha_boarded_third
view 0
report the bystander pilots: ability, secondary, eject (Sneak + J payload is eject)
bystander mark
bystander key ability
wait 30
bystander key eject
wait 30
bystander state
bystander packets sound|particle|actionbar|chat|title|payload

/summon minecraft:cow 6.5 -60 -1.5
bystander fit telekinesis,hop_springs
bystander charge 100000
bystander tp 6.5 -60 1.5 180 20
wait 10

inspect near cow 12
bystander mark
observe 40
bystander key ability
wait 40
observe 30
inspect near cow 12

bystander mark
