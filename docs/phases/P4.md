# Phase 4: Mecha Division

## What's in

| Piece | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Frames** | Hopper (light biped: 60 HP, 6 armour, 350 kg load, 150 kg dry, 400 kFE, heat cap 400) and Bulwark (golem: 140 HP, 12 armour, 800 kg, 320 kg dry, 1.2 MFE, heat cap 800), each with its slot layout (legs, back, core, torso, arms, sensors) |
| **29 modules** | Legs: Hydraulic Legs (required to walk; Stride slider), Spring Heels, Anchor Clamps (sneak to brace), Aqua Fins. Back: Jump Jets (3 mid-air bursts), Rocket Pack (flies only if its thrust, 200-800 kg by slider, beats total mass), Glider Wings, Cargo Hold (27 slots), Passenger Pod, Radiator Fins, Battery Rack. Core: Galvanic Stack (10 FE/t), Combustion Kernel (80 FE/t from fuel in the hold). Torso: Armour Plating (Thickness), Thorn Plating, Fire Suppression, Life Support Recycler, Signal Booster, Solar Skin, Coolant Loop. Arms: Drill (hold; 3x3 head at Torque of 50% or more), Chainsaw (fells whole trees), Hydraulic Fist (10-22 damage by Force), Grapple Hook, Tractor Beam (telekinesis at 24 blocks), Rivet Gun (iron nuggets), Riot Shield (-80% from the front). Sensors: Night Optics, Bio-Scanner |
| **Power, heat, weight** | One FE battery per mecha. Idle and walking drain it; cores refill it. At 0 FE it goes into limp mode (30% speed, modules off). Heat comes from modules, lava and fire. The frame dissipates it: x3 under water, x1.5 in rain, x0.5 in the Nether, plus fins and coolant. At 85% every module is throttled to 70%. At 100% it vents: a 5 s shutdown and a scalding steam cloud, and the pilot takes 2 damage unless the jar has a Heat Shield. Mass against the load rating: full speed up to 60%, falling to 60% speed at 100%, 30% when overloaded, and the servos lock past 125% |
| **Assembly Gantry** | Frame plus modules, validated per slot kind. Assemble (10 kFE) stands the mecha on the pad, owned by you. Take apart pulls a mecha back into the slots. Repair spends Mecha Plating (20 HP each) and tops the battery up from the Gantry. Each module's slider is tuned here. Live mass/load and idle drain |
| **Piloting** (PILOT form) | The same vehicle control as hosts. The mouse buttons drive the left and right arms (the held state is sent to the server). Jump fires the Jump Jets in mid-air. Holding Sneak+J for 1 s ejects; a plain sneak can't leave the cockpit. Hits on the pilot land on the mecha. Golden Parachute: below 10% HP the jar is thrown clear with slow falling. A destroyed mecha pops with no block damage and drops half its modules; the rest comes back as plating. Only the owner and trusted players can board; anyone can ride a Passenger Pod |
| **Protection** | Every block a mecha breaks fires `BlockEvent.BreakEvent` as the pilot, so protection mods and claims apply |
| **Rendering** | Code-defined frame models with named bones. Each module is drawn at its bone from a shared module model. Arms raise while held, the drill spins, and the hull glows red above 60% heat. The chase camera distance scales with frame height |
| **Pilot HUD** | Health, battery, heat, mass/load, the L/R arm modules, and the vent / limp / overloaded / ejecting warnings |

## Deviations from the design

- **GeckoLib** frame models were replaced by vanilla code-defined models with named bones. That avoids a new dependency and keeps the same attachment idea. The Paint Bay, Test Mode, Module Wheel, Target Lock and vision modes are not built.
- Frames other than Hopper and Bulwark (Wheelhouse, Skitter, Chimera, Colossus, Kite, Executive) come in P5/P6, per the build plan.
- Arms beyond the first seven (Manipulator Hand, Excavator Scoop, Flamethrower, Arc Cannon, Hemo-Syringe, Harvester, Welding Torch, Plasma Lance), plus the Energy Shield, Reactive Armour, Gunner Cupola and Co-pilot Socket, come in P6.
- The server charges jet and rocket energy from the mecha's motion, because it never sees the jump key itself.
- The Hopper keeps the design's 350 kg load, so it really is a light frame: legs, an arm, jets and a core already reach 100%.
