## Deviations from the design

- **Seats and sockets.** The Gunner Cupola and Co-pilot Socket (second seats that control weapons) and the Ghost Core (an unpiloted mecha AI) are not built. The Executive's "2 co-pilot sockets" wait for them.
- **Periscope Mast** (a mecha camera mast) is not built; the Collar Cam's Periscope Stalk is.
- **Necrotic Furnace and RTG Pile** are integration modules for Necromancy and HBM; they come in P7.
- **Villager sequencer:** a Villager head in the socket runs a fixed 8-step staircase, not a programmable 8-step GUI.
- **Unstaffed jobs:** a Leatherworker has nothing to staff (the Body Locker already stops decay). The Shepherd's Paint Bay is not built.
- **Executive Jar charge:** charge stays at the server's single `maxCharge`, not the design's 400k per tier.
- **Thermal effects use vanilla Glowing.** Everyone sees the glow, not only the pilot, and it isn't coloured by blood type.
- **Missile Pod** fires a salvo of four on one press.

## Tests

`BoardGameTests` (21) plus everything before: **all 102 pass on the hub**. They cover:

- the frame table, wheel speed off-road, portal size;
- a 16-slot Colossus built at the Gantry, with its shoulder;
- Reactive Armour charges, the Energy Shield's FE cost, a missile salvo and utility cycling;
- turret targeting rules, a skeleton turret using arrows;
- Cerebral Array output (per head, the Board Meeting bonus, the Dragon Head with and without the core, broth);
- unpaid staff at half bonus, payroll once a day;
- the socket's six channels and pulse, the zombie-head clock;
- an entangled link across dimensions (and refused for a plain or mismatched pair), Quantum Goggles at 50,000 blocks, the EMP breaking a link but not a shielded one;
- four collar upgrades;
- dragon's breath DNA, Decay Aura;
- the Lacrimal Laser's cost and cooldown;
- the Executive Jar's tier.

The hidden-client script (`tools/smoke-p6.txt`) showed:

- all eight frames side by side;
- the 16-slot Gantry with a Colossus;
- a turret, the Cerebral Array with nine heads racked on its face, a staffed sequencer, a socket running a zombie clock;
- the Array screen;
- the socket HUD with six channels.
