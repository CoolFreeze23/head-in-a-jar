## Deviations from the design

- **Necromancy** (Minions as hosts, souls, the Necrotic Furnace, the Ghost Core) waits for a Necromancy build in the pack. Its jar isn't in the mods folder.
- **Not built:**
  - Shoulder Surfing, Walkers, Controlify, SecurityCraft, JourneyMap and the HBM/RTG stretch items.
  - The ProjectE EMC blacklist and the Carry On blacklist; these belong in the pack's config files.
- **JEI** shows information pages rather than custom recipe categories, because the machines aren't recipe-driven.
- **Jade** shows what the client already knows. Machine progress and staff pay status would need server data providers.
- **Conditions:** the compat host files carry `neoforge:conditions`, but this loader reads all 93 files anyway; the extra ones are inert.
- **Art pass:** the mecha are still code models, not GeckoLib ones, and the Turret's blaze head still shows as rods. The "four angles of every frame and module" screenshots were not taken; the P6 line-up covers the frames from the front.
- **Balance:** no measured balance pass yet; the numbers are the design's.

- `everyLangKeyInBothLanguages`: the same 715 keys in en_us and pt_br, none blank, and every gene named;
- all 25 advancements load, and the trigger grants one;
- all nine sounds are registered;

- Regrowth's last stand and cooldown, Mule Fire and Pachyderm Hide, the Blazing Soul burst, Atomic Breath's fire and cooldown.
