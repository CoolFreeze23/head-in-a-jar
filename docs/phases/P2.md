# Phase 2: Life Support

## What's in

| Piece | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Cranial Saline** fluid | real fluid (type, source/flowing, block, bucket, teal tint over vanilla water); `ModFluids` helper is reused by later fluids |
| **Saline Mixer** | 1000 mB water + sugar + kelp + redstone + 2,000 FE over 10 s → 1000 mB saline; bottles saline (250 mB per glass bottle); water in / saline out through pipes |
| **Jar life support** | per-jar saline (1000/1500/2000 mB) and **Jar Integrity** (10/20/30) synced to the client. A head out in the world uses 1 mB per 12 s (config); docked and carried heads use none; the **Saline Recycler** halves it. A dry jar loses 1 integrity per 30 s. Integrity soaks up damage before health, and blows to a placed Head Jar hit it. A placed head that dries out completely is a real death |
| **Refills** | Saline Bottle (+250 mB) and **Glass Patch** (+5 integrity), fed through the Jar menu or used on your own placed head |
| **Cradle upkeep** | 50 kFE buffer and 4000 mB saline tank (bottles, buckets, pipes). It refills a docked head's saline and charge, gives +1 integrity every 10 s, and uses 5 FE/t |
| **Leyden Jar** | 250 kFE battery; feeds every neighbour except other Leyden Jars |
| **Tesla Node** + **Surgical Wrench** | wireless FE, 2,000 FE/t up to 16 blocks with 5% loss; link the sender, then the receiver; an unlinked node feeds its neighbours; arcs once a second |
| **Relay Mast / Mk II** | radius 96 / 192, 1 FE/t. Links route head → masts → body; a path is as good as its weakest hop, less 3% per mast. Masts keep relaying while unloaded until their stored energy would have run out (`RelayRegistry`) |
| **Signal Jammer** | 40 FE/t, cuts strangers' links within 16 blocks to 30%; owner and trusted players immune |
| **Relay Goggles Mk II / III** + **Synapse Chip** | 160 / 640 blocks; fit them by using them on your own placed or docked head (the old pair comes back) |
| **Body Printer** | 16 rotten flesh, 8 bone blocks, 4 redstone blocks, 8 slime balls, 2000 mB saline, 200 kFE over 60 s; only a jar can press Print; the new body (Collar Cam fitted) becomes the jar's body, an old corpse stays with its belongings |
| **Nine new modules** | Suction Cups (wall climbing), Spider Legs (walk 80%, step 1 block), Speaker Grille (chat floats over the jar), Saline Recycler, Shock Lid (3 damage to melee attackers, 500 FE), Self-Righting Gyro (knockback immune, no upside-down view), Hub Antenna (+50% range), Life Sign Monitor (body health on the HUD), Hat Mount (helmet drawn on the lid) |
| **Head-operable machines** | a bare jar can work the mod's own machines (tag `headinajar:head_operable`) but nothing else |
| **Safety Placards** | wall notices, 12 variants cycled by use, each read out in chat |
| **Employee Handbook** | Patchouli book skeleton (4 categories, 8 entries) in en_us and pt_br |
| **Shared machine GUI** | `MachineMenu` + `MachineScreen`: energy bar, fluid gauges, progress arrow, buttons, all drawn without textures |

## Deviations from the design

- **Body Printer** uses slime balls + saline instead of Bio-Gel (Bio-Gel comes with genetics in P5; the recipe will switch then).
- **Cradle GUI** (swap/jar-cam/release buttons) not built; using it shows energy and saline in chat, and the owner docks/undocks by hand.
- **Relay Mast** is a single tall-model block, not a 1x1x3 multiblock.
- Leyden Jar Mk II/III (2M/16M) wait for their tiers (P4+).
- Lacrimal Laser, Cortex Coprocessor and Body Recall Beacon (T3 modules) are left for P6.

## Tests

`LifeSupportGameTests` (12) plus P1 (15) and v1 (12): **all 39 pass on the hub**. The hidden-client smoke script (`tools/smoke-p2.txt`) checked 53 items and 86 block states for models and names and showed the machines, placards, saline pool, the Mixer GUI and the jar HUD with charge, saline, integrity and the Life Sign Monitor.
