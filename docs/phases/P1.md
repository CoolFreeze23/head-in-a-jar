## Deviations from the design

- **Booth:** the single-block Separation Chair the design allows as the phase-1 fallback. The 3x3x4 multiblock with
  ports and the hologram is not built.
- **Saline:** a bottle item only. Four bottles stand in for 1000 mB. The fluid, its drain and the Saline Mixer are P2.
- **Galvanic Pile:** moved up from P2, because the pack has no FE generator and the chair needs 40 kFE.
- **Charge:** v1 numbers x100, now FE (100 kFE jar). No old-save migration, since nothing was released.
- **Body Printer:** not built yet (P2). Until then a Suture Kit used as a jar on your corpse revives it with its
  belongings and 3 hearts, so a dead body is never a dead end.
- **Head Satchel:** an inventory item, not a Curios belt slot (Curios is P7).
- **Head on logout:** a placed head stays in the world and is invulnerable while its owner is offline. The design's
  "lift the entity on logout, restore it on login" isn't needed with that rule.
- **Mod name:** the creative tab says "Head Office". The mod id stays `headinajar`, and the jar/display name in pt_br stays "Cabeça no Pote".

## Known gaps carried forward

- Jar Integrity (the second health pool) arrives with life support in P2. A jar still takes damage to player health.
- Not yet: the Jar Cam key, Cradle GUI, Speed/Anaesthetic/Precision/Auto-Dock cassettes, Surgical Wrench and multiblock.
- Carrying another (trusting) player's head: P3, with villager heads.

## Tests

`HeadStartGameTests` (15) plus v1's `JarGameTests` (12): **all 27 pass on the hub** (2026-11-08).

The hidden-client smoke script (`tools/smoke-p1.txt`) checked that 30 items and 9 block states have models and names. It
signed the waiver in a loaded chair and played the whole operation. Screenshots show the gas, the scan line, black,
and the "NEURO-LINK 2.1" boot static. It then checked the linked body with the Collar Cam from front and back, and
the link HUD at 31 m reading 71% (the formula says 71). It swapped into the jar and back.

Bugs the tests and screenshots caught and fixed:
- the chair's own restraints stopped it standing the patient up at the end;
- an emptied Cradle sent an empty update, which the client skips, so clients kept drawing the departed head;
- sync packets sent to fake or mock players threw (fixed in v1: only channel-negotiated connections get them).
