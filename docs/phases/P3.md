# Phase 3: Hostile Takeover

## What's in

| Piece | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Surgical Saw** | on a mob below 25% health: it becomes a **host** (its mind switches off, its head is hidden, it never despawns, not even on Peaceful). With an empty Life Support Jar in the off hand, the saw keeps the **head** instead and the body falls. Refuses bosses (`#c:bosses`), named mobs, other players' pets, hosts and anything in `headinajar:not_a_host`. It acts before the mob's own use, so villagers don't open trades |
| **Piloting** (`vehicle` control) | a jar uses a host to climb onto its neck and becomes **HOSTED**. A linked body carrying its head moves into the host and leaves the body standing. Mixins hand any mob's control to its jar (horse, pig, strider and camel riding rules included): rider input, steering, the host's own speed, jumping, fliers rising on jump and sinking on sneak, climbers climbing. Sneak leaves the host; a dead host drops the jar. Attacks are made by the host (`doHurtTarget`), at the host's reach |
| **Host data files** | `data/<ns>/headinajar/host/<entity>.json`: neck offset, blood type, decay rate, attributes, flags (`fire_immune`, `water_breathing`, `climb`, `slow_fall`, `no_fall_damage`, `night_vision`, `knockback_immune`, `flight`, `speed_aura`), drawbacks, `neutral_to`, primary/secondary ability. 41 vanilla creatures + 3 shells. A creature without a file still works with its own attributes. Neck offsets are synced to clients |
| **Abilities** | 17 types: teleport, explode, fireball (small or large), arrows, web (melts after 10 s), charge/pounce, toss, roar, lay_egg, ink, wind_charge, sonic_boom, potion, groan (nearby kin attack your target), echolocation, hover, sprint. R is the primary (50% sync), G the secondary (75%), each with its cooldown |
| **Freshness** | 2%/min (per file); <50% Stiff (-15% speed), <25% Ripe (-30%, flies); at 0 the host falls apart (normal loot) and the jar pops out. **Embalming Fluid** refreshes to 100% and halves decay for a day; **Neural Lace** (sync ≥ 50%) stops decay for good; the **Body Locker** stores a host frozen in time |
| **Sync** | +1% per 12 s of piloting; 25% traits, 50% primary, 75% secondary, 100% +10% health/damage/speed. Under 25% the host is clumsy (input drops now and then) |
| **Neural Handshake** | first mount: a ring closes on a target three times; press R as it lines up (+3% sync per hit, Nausea per miss). Client option to skip it (+5% flat) |
| **Jarred heads** | any mob's head keeps the whole mob record; placed as a block it shows the mob's real head (its own model and texture) floating in the jar. A **jarred villager still trades** (stand-in villager, saved back after trading, restocks twice a day). Breaking it returns the head with everything |
| **Utility Shells** | Lunk (60 HP, 9 attack, knockback immune, Toss), Rover (fast, 9-slot cargo by sneak-use, Sprint), Spider Walker (climbs, no fall damage, Web Shot): robot hosts that never decay, are fully synced, and run on the jar's charge (20 FE/t) |
| Host HUD | host health, freshness, sync, and which ability keys are unlocked |

## Deviations from the design

- **Shape control** (fliers as the player's own shape) is not built: fliers (bat, phantom, ghast) use vehicle control with jump/sneak for up/down.
- **Necromancy Minions** as hosts, **OreSpawn** and **Brazil Legends** host files: moved to P7 (cross-mod pass; Necromancy isn't in the pack).
- **Host blood samples on collapse** come with genetics (P5).
- Passive "pick up any block" for endermen, the warden's blindness drawback and a few per-creature drawbacks are left as flavour for the balance pass.

## Tests

`HostGameTests` (12) plus P1/P2/v1: **all 51 pass on the hub**. The hidden-client scripts (`tools/smoke-p3.txt`, `tools/smoke-p3b.txt`) showed headless hosts (cow, enderman, spider, wolf), the three shells, a jarred villager with its face in the jar, the handshake ring, and a jar riding a cow with the host HUD.

Bugs caught on the way: the neck seat missed `LivingEntity`'s override; a host in its death animation still counted as a host; the enderman model forces its head visible every frame (heads are now skipped at draw time); Peaceful deleted monster hosts.
